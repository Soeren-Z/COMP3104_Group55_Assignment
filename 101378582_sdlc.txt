DevOps plays an important role in the software development lifecycle (SDLC) by combining development and operations. It ensures faster delivery, fewer bugs, and smoother deployments.
DevOps is integrated into the software development lifecycle (SDLC) to improve collaboration between development and operations teams.
In the requirements and testing phases, DevOps ensures continuous feedback and automated testing to catch issues early.
During deployment and maintenance, DevOps automates releases and monitors systems to ensure reliable performance.
