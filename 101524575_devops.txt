COMP3104 (DevOps) is a course at George Brown College that introduces students to the essential practices and tools used by modern DevOps engineers. 
Through a mix of lectures, hands-on labs, and relevant assignments, students learn about continuous integration, deployment strategies, version control workflows, and cloud computing platforms. 
The curriculum covers both technical skills like automating deployment pipelines and collaboration techniques for effective teamwork.
