George Brown College is located in Toronto, Ontario. It features three campuses, St. James Campus, Casa Loma
Campus, and Waterfront Campus. George Brown College offers a wide range of programs to help develop the skills
employers are looking for. To help develop these skills GBC has partnered with many well known national and
international organizations that offer a broad range of experience and knowledge for students. GBC has a focus on
experiential learning offering students the ability to gain sector specific experience for their program.
