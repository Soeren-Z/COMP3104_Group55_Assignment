COMP 3104 focuses on teaching students the proper procedures, protocols and best practices that a DevOps engineer
needs for the workplace. This knowledge aims to ensure that the students brings relevant knowledge that can
increase productivity in a workplace environment. This course has students analyze and evaluate developer operation
implementations available and implement hands on work using industry standard tools. Students will create solutions
to complex business problems by adhering to core principles and responsibilities of DevOps.

The outcomes of this course have the students reliably demonstrate the ability to analyze and utilize version
control systems workflows, make well informed architectural decisions for software, demonstrate knowledge critical
to DevOps technique like continuous integration/development, and ensure effective collaboration on software
projects. Ultimately this course aims to have students learn to be effective DevOps engineers.
